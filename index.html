En ese camino trabajé de cerca con Data Scientists para llevar sus modelos a producción, y hoy me dedico a eso como Machine Learning Engineer en NTT DATA.

I started out automating processes and building dashboards on Google Cloud, then moved on to designing Lakehouse pipelines on Azure and Databricks for BCP. I trained in Data Science at Le Wagon on a ProInnovate scholarship and keep learning on my own. I enjoy writing the pipeline as much as teaching a team how to use it.
